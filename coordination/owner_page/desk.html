Two findings from the measurement (worth your eye)

  • A cut read is silent. When a single text is too big, the harness returns a partial read with no notice in the tool result (this version). A session that did not notice would act on half a text. The guard is keeping the single-text limit well below the cut — both 24,000 and 60,000 are. It revises what design part 7 §2 assumed; I can note that in the design on your word.
  • A headless session cannot see its own fill. One reader guessed 70% when it was 16%. Refreshing at 40% relies on reading the fill level; that works in an interactive session but not a headless one (it guessed early, the safe way). It touches design part 7 §1 and how unattended refresh works; I'll bring you options if you want them.
