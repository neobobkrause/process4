The options.

  • C — Join the two histories (recommended). ARCH makes one branch from main whose content is exactly p4-tranche's, except your approvals file and the forbidden-commands file, which keep main's newer versions. Nothing is rerun, nothing is deleted, your branch rule is untouched, and from then on the tranche goes on from p4-tranche and future merges into main work.   • A — No change. The work stays off main.
