# The record of the owner's decisions about the Process4 design

PROCESS is the only writer of this record (design part 5, section 5). It is kept
in the process's repository and kept up to date with the design document in the
same step as each decision is recorded (design part 5, section 4).

## How this record works

- It is **ordered by subject**. Each entry states the rule as it now stands.
- A decision that **changes an entry rewrites it in place.** There is one entry
  for each subject, not a running log.
- The owner's **exact dated words are kept and never edited.** They are quoted in
  the entry, with the date he typed them.
- A **rewritten entry is shown to the owner once before it stands.**
- Only the owner's words count as his decision. A message of his is a decision or
  an instruction only when it begins "Decision:" or "Instruction:". Everything
  else is conversation (design part 1, section 4). PROCESS writes no summary of
  his words as his.
- This record holds his decisions about the **design**. His decisions about a
  **product** are kept by that product's coordinator, in the product's own
  repository (design part 5, section 5).

## The shape of an entry

Each entry is written in this shape. The example below is made up, to show the
shape only; it is not a real decision.

> ### Subject: (a short name for the subject)
>
> **The rule as it now stands.** One or two plain sentences stating the current
> rule for this subject.
>
> **The owner's words.** "Decision: ..." — (date he typed it).
>
> (If a later decision changed this subject, the rule above is rewritten in
> place and the new dated words are added here, below the earlier ones. His
> earlier words are kept, never edited.)

## No decisions are recorded here yet

This record is newly set up and holds no entries. The owner's design decisions
made while Process4 was being written are, as the design states, kept as his
typed words in the process3 repository, under `process4/owner_words/`, with the
reasons and evidence in `process4/evidence/` and `process4/working/` (design
part 5, section 7).

Migrating those existing dated decisions into this record — one entry per
subject, in his exact words — is a separate, careful piece of work. PROCESS will
do it only on the owner's word, because the words quoted here must be his exact
typed words and must never be fabricated or paraphrased. Until then, the design
document (`design/`) remains the statement of the design as it stands, and this
record carries the owner's decisions from this point forward.
